Key aspects/feelings that I'm trying to evoke in this game:
	
	Chance of getting good items
	Overwhelming strength to demolish enemies (borderline gamebreaking)
	Cooperation and flow under pressure
		Need a goal to achieve - beat the boss
			Build a ship that can beat the boss
			Collect components
			Destroy enemies
		Battling enemies should be fun
			Identifying what the enemy is capable of and employing the approriate countermeasures
			Identify -> Decide (avoid/destroy) -> Setup -> Act
